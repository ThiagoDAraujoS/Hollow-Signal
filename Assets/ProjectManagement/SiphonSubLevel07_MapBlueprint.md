## Detailed Zone & Obstacle Interactions

### 1. Zone 1: Intake Hallway (Bottom-Left)
* **Visuals:** Dark riveted steel walls, industrial cage light, iron desk with paperwork.
* **Challenge:** `SuspiciousSentry` (Inspector Vane).
* **Approaches:**
  - `Parley`: Flash forged credentials or cite municipal emergency codes.
  - `Sneak`: Slip behind his blind angle toward the ventilation duct.
  - `Force`: Tackle or disarm the guard.

### 2. Chokepoint: Iron Portcullis & Ventilation Duct
* **Visuals:** Barred portcullis chained shut, adjacent to an overhead square ventilation duct.
* **Challenge:** `SecurityGate`.
* **Approaches:**
  - `Lockpick`: Pick the cylinder tumblers directly on the gate.
  - `Sneak`: Shimmy through the overhead sheet-metal ventilation duct.
  - `Pry`: Lever the chained locking rods loose with a prybar.

### 3. Zone 2: Flooded Manifold Catwalk
* **Visuals:** Elevated steel walkway suspended over a canal of glowing toxic violet runoff. A massive pipe with a seized valve wheel blocks the walking path.
* **Challenge:** `SeizedValve`.
* **Approaches:**
  - `Pry`: Wedge a spanner between the wheel spokes and heave.
  - `Tinker`: Bleed the corroded pressure collar to ease rotation.
  - `Force`: Grip the frozen wheel with raw muscular exertion.

### 4. Zone 3: Arcane Relay Hallway
* **Visuals:** Stone archway inlaid with glowing yellow-metal runes; crackling violet electricity arcing between brass capacitor coils across the doorway.
* **Challenge:** `RunicFlicker`.
* **Approaches:**
  - `Tinker`: Ground the high-voltage leads using insulated pliers.
  - `Decipher`: Read the precursor inversion symbols to identify the safe frequency phase.
  - `Force`: Sever the primary power feed with a heavy blow.

### 5. Zone 4: Secure Control Vault & Exit Hatch (Top-Right)
* **Visuals:** Fortified control room with brass analog dials, vacuum-tube gauges, a locked iron wall safe, and a heavy circular vault exit hatch.
* **Challenge:** `ClassifiedRecord`.
* **Approaches:**
  - `Decipher`: Decode the cipher lock or encrypted logbook.
  - `Tinker`: Manipulate the mechanical tumbler dial.
  - `Parley`: Interrogate or negotiate with the station operator.
