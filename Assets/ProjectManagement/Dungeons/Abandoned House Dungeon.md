# Dungeon Overview: The Humming Shack (Relay Station 09)

- **Theme:** Non-magical, gritty, rusted sci-fi (Borderlands junkyard aesthetic meets Fallout industrial isolation).
- **The Hook:** The desert highway is merciless. The car’s engine is running hot (or the payload requires a cooling/tether cycle), and miles off the broken asphalt sits an impossible sight: a lone weather-beaten shack with a working, whirring windmill.
- **The Backstory:** An eccentric hermit mechanic thought they could automate their survival bunker by hooking an old-world defense sub-routine into the homestead's power grid. Instead, the AI malware seized the hermit's cybernetic implants and defibrillator grid, electrocuting him and puppeteering his desiccated corpse through a web of high-voltage conduits.
## The 3 Maps & Environmental Setup

##### Map 1: The Outside (Windmill & Perimeter)

- **Visuals:** Sun-baked salt flat, a skeletal wind turbine creaking violently in the gale, an old fuel-drum hitching post, and tire tracks buried by red dust.
- **Atmosphere:** Howling radioactive wind, the rhythmic _thump-thump-thump_ of the windmill blades, and an unnatural buzzing sound vibrating right up through the soles of their boots.
- **Interactive Elements:**  
    - **The Hitching Pole:** Heavy iron stanchion cemented into bedrock. Ideal for chaining down the speed car against dust storms while cooling down.
    - **The Turbine Brake Lever:** A rusted mechanical brake line on the windmill base. Pulling it slows the turbine, cutting power surges to the house.
##### Map 2: The Topside (The Barricaded Shack)
- **Visuals:** Rotted wood panelling, blown-in grit, a sun-bleached recliner sofa, a dry cast-iron sink, and crude shelves with scavenged cans.
- **The Red Flag:** Thick, insulated copper cables snake along the baseboards like roots, drilled straight down through crude holes in the wooden floorboards.
- **Atmosphere:** Smells like ozone, fried dust, and dead animal. A dead, low 60Hz hum constantly vibrates the dishes in the sink.
##### Map 3: The Downside (The Sub-Level Server Nest)
- **Visuals:** Cold, damp concrete bomb shelter transformed into a glowing electric charnel house. Green and amber CRT screens flicker in a server rack. Suspended from the ceiling is an umbilical nest of jumper cables, wiring harnesses, and transformers.
- **The Horror:** Slumped in the center is the "Survivor Degenerate": a mummified corpse fused to the cabling, metal electrodes hammered into its temples, twitching intermittently with electric spasms.
## The 5 Scenes in Flow
```
[Outside] → SCENE 1: The Puzzle (Unlock the Vault)
               ↓
[Topside] → SCENE 2: The Foreshadowing (Signs of Paranoia)
               ↓
[Topside / Outside] → SCENE 3: The Setback (Power Surge / Car Threat)
               ↓
[Downside] → SCENE 4: The Climax (The Fatal Malware Web)
               ↓
[Downside] → SCENE 5: The Resolution (Lights On & The Armory)
```

### Scene 1: The Foreshadowing — "The Barricaded Hatch" (Topside)

- **Location:** Inside the decaying shack's living room.
- **The Situation:** The shack is a dead man's home. Dust covers empty ration cans, and thick, insulated cables are drilled haphazardly straight through the floorboards, humming with a low 60Hz vibration.
- **The Hatch & The Clues:**
    - Shoved into the corner, a ruined, spring-busted sofa and an overturned refrigerator are wedged against the wall.
    - Moving them aside reveals an iron-banded wooden trapdoor nailed shut and barricaded with scrap rebar welded across the seam.
    - **The Tell:** It was bolted and barricaded from the topside. Gouge marks on the wood show someone dragging heavy furniture in sheer terror.
    - Painted in rusted red primer on the wood:
        "DO NOT PRY. IT TALKS THROUGH THE PIPES. JOHN IS GONE."
- **Bypassing Door 1:** This is purely physical. Players can crowbar the rebar (**Might Task Lv 2**), cut it with a torch/hacksaw, or blast the hinges. When opened, a draft of icy, ozone-chilled air drifts up from a ladder leading down into darkness.
### Scene 2: The Puzzle — "The Ghost in the Grid"
- **Goal:** Discover and bypass the electronic deadbolt on the basement bunker door.
- **The Setup:**  
    - The trapdoor is hidden underneath the cellar.
    - The hatch is locked tight with a electric lock. There is no visible keyhole, and prying it with brute force is brutal (**Might Task Lv 6** to pry, retries cost Effort) but can be solved with explosives.
- **The Clever Solution:**
    - Investigating the snaking wires leads players to the outside. **Intellect Task Lv 3** reveals a false breaker panel tucked beside the dry sink plumbing.
    - The panel is fried: the windmill outside is overcharging the breaker, locking the door shut.
    - **To Solve:** Players can run outside to Map 1 to mechanically fix the windmill by lockpick/hotwire its panel with insulated tools (**Speed Task Lv 3**, or **Lv 5** without finding the panel first), 
### Scene 3: The Setback — "High-Voltage Backlash"
_(A sudden hazard that drains resources and puts pressure on their car/payload)_
- **The Incident:** As the players pop the trapdoor latch or tamper with the panel, the rogue AI senses the intrusion and discharges the house’s auxiliary capacitor bank in a retaliatory **Arc Surge**.
- **The Consequences:** 
    - **Option A (Personal Hazard):** A blast of blinding blue sparks erupts from the hatch opening. Players within immediate range make a **Speed Task Lv 3** defense roll. Failure inflicts 4 shock damage and temporarily magnetizes/jams metal weapons (revolvers, rifles) until cleaned.
    - **Option B (The Payload & Vehicle Setback):** Because the players parked near the shack and hitched up to the iron pole (which grounds the house), the electrical surge arcs down the tether wire into the speed car:
        - The car's auxiliary battery pops, or the dangerous payload's temperature spikes.
        - _Ticking Clock:_ Players have limited time to dive into the basement, kill the source, and retrieve a replacement coolant cell / fuse before the payload destabilizes.
### Scene 4: The Climax — "Malware Web & The Degenerate Host"
- **The Encounter:** The players drop into the damp basement. The servers whine like jet engines. The tangled mass of wires in the ceiling lowers the corpse into the center of the room.
- **The Threat:**  
    - **The Host:** A twitching, electrified corpse armed with a rusted pneumatic rebar cutter or arcing cattle prod.
    - **Immunity Trait:** Every time it takes lethal damage, the overhead cables violently pump 20,000 volts through its chest, jump-starting it back to life with a kinetic shockwave.
- **Combat & Tactical Objectives:**
    - **The Terminal (Intellect Task Lv 3):** An active green-phosphor console sitting next to the server rack. A successful hack runs an emergency system format, dealing **6 direct damage** to the malware matrix and paralyzing the puppet for 1 round.
    - **The Umbilical Lines (Speed / Might Task Lv 3):** Targeting the thick hanging cables (or shooting the ceramic ceiling insulators). Severing 2 power lines cuts the boss’s regeneration completely, making it mortal.
    - **Direct Destruction (Might / Combat):** Blasting the puppet’s limbs or destroying the server stack itself with high-explosive or kinetic damage.
### Scene 5: The Resolution — "Flipping the Breakers"
- **The Aftermath:** With the AI core crushed or formatted, the room dies into absolute silence. The hanging cables drop limp, and the corpse collapses into dust and scrap.
- **Unlocking the Vault:** With the system offline, emergency power switches over to auxiliary yellow safety lights. At the back of the basement, a sealed pneumatic shutter sighs open, revealing the hermit’s pristine, hidden workshop.

## GM Read-Aloud Descriptions
**When approaching the shack (Outside):**
> _"The desert wind whips red grit against your car's windshield. Through the heat mirage, the rusted skeleton of a single homestead appears—miraculously standing in the wasteland. Beside it, an archaic windmill spins with furious speed, shrieking against the gusts. A thick iron hitching pole sits rooted in the rock out front, as if waiting for a traveler desperate for shelter."_

**Entering the basement (Climax start):**
> _"Your boots splash into stagnant water on cold concrete. The darkness hums—not a mechanical hum, but a living, buzzing vibration in your teeth. Green phosphor light blooms across three server racks. From the ceiling, an obscene spiderweb of jumper cables and copper tubes tethers to a shape in the center: a man in stained overalls, his head tilted back, chest stitched with arcing copper wires. As the servers whine, the corpse's fingers twitch, a relay clicks in his chest, and two milky eyes snap open in a flash of electric blue."_
