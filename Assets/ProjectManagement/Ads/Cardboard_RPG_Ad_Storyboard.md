# Hollow Signal — Ad Storyboard: "Cardboard RPG"

**Target Duration:** ~15–18 seconds  
**Format:** 9:16 Vertical (TikTok / Reels / Shorts) + crop-safe for 16:9 (Twitter / Steam)  
**Concept:** Live-action physical puppetry parodying generic turn-based RPG combat, subverting expectations with tactile comedy and an escalating physical punchline.

---

## 1. High Concept & Core Hook

- **The Hook:** A giant, physical cardboard mouse cursor swoops into a cardboard tabletop diorama, breaking the fourth wall immediately.
- **The Contrast:** Sets up a classic turn-based attack (clunky bonk, red number 5), promises a "revolutionary" alternative, pauses on a choice menu, and delivers an absurd tactile payoff.
- **The Escalation:** Choosing "Hack" doesn't just zap the robot with blue damage—the robot vibrates frantically and dispenses a real, heavy physical soda can onto the table.
- **The Payoff / CTA:** A mechanical roll-down cardboard banner drops down: **WISHLIST ON STEAM**. The giant cursor nudges it straight.

---

## 2. Props & Build List (DIY Low-Budget Craft)

| Prop | Description / Construction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cardboard Hero** | 2D cardboard cutout on a popsicle stick or wooden skewer. Expressive, slightly deadpan pose. |
| **Cardboard Robot** | 2D cutout on a stick. Features a pre-cut square flap in the torso hinged with tape at the bottom. |
| **Giant Mouse Cursor** | Oversized OS mouse pointer cut from corrugated cardboard, mounted on a stiff wooden dowel or ruler held off-screen. |
| **Action Dropdown Menu** | A small cardboard dialogue / popup card with bold hand-drawn buttons: `[ ATTACK ]` and `[ HACK ]`. Can be held on a stick or flipped down on a hinge. |
| **Floating Numbers** | Red paper cutout `5` and Blue paper cutout `5`, attached to transparent fishing line or thin floral wire to make them "float" upward. |
| **Physical Soda Can** | Real, chilled 330ml/12oz aluminum beverage can hidden behind the robot prop until launched. |
| **Roll-Down CTA Banner** | Kraft paper or cardboard banner rolled around a small wooden dowel/cardboard tube, suspended by two pieces of twined string. Marked with: **"WISHLIST ON STEAM"** + *Hollow Signal* logo. |

## 3. Shot-by-Shot Visual & Audio Storyboard

### **Beat 1: The Status Quo (0:00 – 0:04)**
- **Framing:** Tabletop stage view. Plain neutral backdrop (or hand-drawn cardboard wasteland ruins). The Hero and Robot cutouts stand opposite each other.
- **Visual Action:**
  1. The giant cardboard mouse cursor swoops into the frame from the top-right.
  2. Cursor hovers over the robot; someone holds a red cardboard bracket around the robot (highlight).
  3. The cursor visibly presses down into the robot with an exaggerated click.
  4. The Hero cutout is jerked forward by hand and bonks the Robot with a flat *thwack*.
  5. A red cardboard **`5`** floats upward on a thin wire.
- **Audio & SFX:**
  - Cardboard scraping / rustling on the table.
  - Exaggerated mechanical mouse click (*CLACK!*).
  - Dry, dull cardboard thud.
  - Low-key, generic 8-bit battle music loop in the background.
- **Voiceover / On-Screen Text:**
  - *VO (casual, slightly mocking):* "Do you want to play an RPG more interesting than this?"
  - *Subtitles:* Large, punchy white text across the middle.

---

### **Beat 2: The Choice & The Dilemma (0:04 – 0:08)**
- **Framing:** Slight digital zoom-in or camera tilt toward the Robot.
- **Visual Action:**
  1. The giant cursor enters again and hovers over the robot.
  2. An action menu card flips down or slides in from the side:
     ```
     ┌──────────────┐
     │  ▶ ATTACK    │
     │  ▶ HACK      │
     └──────────────┘
     ```
  3. The cursor hovers on `ATTACK`... pauses... shakes back and forth... then hovers over `HACK`.
  4. Cursor eagerly double-taps `HACK`.
- **Audio & SFX:**
  - Fast cardboard swoosh / paper slide sound.
  - Tense silence (music cuts out for dramatic anticipation).
  - Crisp, sharp double-click sound (*Click-click!*).
- **Voiceover / On-Screen Text:**
  - *VO:* "Instead of that... how about this?"

---

### **Beat 3: The Escalation & The Can Drop (0:08 – 0:13)**
- **Framing:** Stage view focusing on the interaction between the Hero and the Robot.
- **Visual Action:**
  1. The Hero cutout slides over and gently pokes the Robot with a single finger cutout.
  2. The Robot cutout is shaken violently by the puppeteer's hand.
  3. A blue cardboard **`5`** floats up.
  4. **The Twist:** The tape flap on the Robot's chest abruptly pops open.
  5. The off-screen puppeteer shoves a **real, heavy aluminum soda can** through the flap.
  6. The can tumbles out and hits the wooden tabletop with a loud, real-world clatter and rolls slightly.
  7. The Hero cutout tilts its head down to stare at the soda can. The mouse cursor freezes in mid-air.
- **Audio & SFX:**
  - Comic sci-fi electrical buzzer / fry sound (*BZZZZT-ZAP!*).
  - Melodic retro synth chime for the blue `5`.
  - **LOUD REAL-WORLD FOLEY:** Heavy metal can slamming onto wood (*CLATTER-RATTLE!*) + fizzy carbonated *pssshh* sound effect.
  - Complete silence / stunned ambient hum for 0.5s.
- **Voiceover / On-Screen Text:**
  - *VO:* Dead silence, followed by a dry: "...Huh." (or no voiceover at all, letting the physical gag breathe).

---

### **Beat 4: The Roll-Down Call to Action (0:13 – 0:17)**
- **Framing:** Full frame showing the stage, the soda can resting on the desk, and the characters.
- **Visual Action:**
  1. From above the camera frame, the rolled cardboard banner drops down on two strings like a mini classroom projector screen.
  2. It unrolls briskly, displaying:
     ```
     ┌───────────────────────────────┐
     │         HOLLOW SIGNAL         │
     │      ★ WISHLIST ON STEAM ★    │
     └───────────────────────────────┘
     ```
  3. The banner hangs slightly crooked at first.
  4. The giant cardboard mouse cursor gently re-enters from the bottom-right, taps the crooked corner upward to straighten it, and gives a quick little cardboard "thumbs-up" or nod before leaving.
- **Audio & SFX:**
  - Paper unrolling / string squeak (*fwwrrrump*).
  - Light, charming cardboard tap (*tap*).
  - Punchy, upbeat closing synth chord or lo-fi bass sting.
- **Voiceover / On-Screen Text:**
  - *VO (cheerful / warm):* "Wishlist Hollow Signal on Steam today."

---

## 4. Foley & Sound Design Notes

The humor of this ad relies heavily on the **juxtaposition between flat low-budget cardboard and heavy tactile realism**:
1. **Clicks:** Use a real mouse clicked right up against the microphone, or a loud mechanical keyboard switch.
2. **Pokes/Bonks:** Tap a real cardboard box with a wooden pencil or stick.
3. **The Soda Can:** Must sound surprisingly heavy and loud compared to the lightweight paper elements. A genuine *thud-clatter* creates instant comedic weight.
4. **Music:** 
   - 0:00–0:04: Cheesy, lo-fi 8-bit turn-based battle theme.
   - 0:04–0:08: Cuts out completely for the agonizing cursor decision.
   - 0:08–0:13: Funny electric buzz, then instant drop back to absolute silence when the can drops.
   - 0:13–0:17: Cool, stylized retro synth-wave sting that matches the true tone of *Hollow Signal*.

## 5. Production & Filming Checklist

- [ ] Print and cut out Hero & Robot artwork onto brown corrugated cardboard / cardstock.
- [ ] Mount characters on popsicle sticks / skewers.
- [ ] Cut the trapdoor on the Robot prop and hinge it with duct tape / masking tape.
- [ ] Craft the giant cursor out of cardboard with a handle long enough to keep your arm off-camera.
- [ ] Rig the roll-down banner with two strings attached to a wooden skewer / dowel.
- [ ] Position camera on a tripod at table level with warm desk lamp lighting to give a cozy, workshop feel.
- [ ] Do 2–3 dry runs of launching the soda can through the flap to ensure it rolls cleanly forward without knocking over the whole stage set.
