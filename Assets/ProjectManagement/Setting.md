# Setting Bible: The Plane of Bren & The Great Cosmic Lie

> **Project:** Hollow Signal (CRPG)  
> **Document Purpose:** Definitive setting guide, societal architecture, cosmological lore, and engine integration specifications for LLM context parsing and narrative design.  
> **Primary Location:** The City of Bren / The Waste of Bren  
> **Genre:** Dieselpunk / Arcanepunk / Cosmic Horror CRPG  

---

## 1. Quick-Reference System Metadata & World Axioms

```yaml
Plane Name: Bren
Dominant Cosmology: Hard Lawful Evil (Mechanical, Predatory, Ineffable)
Planar Atmosphere: Perpetual Purple Twilight (Fluctuating luminance, zero diurnal cycle)
Primary Environmental State: Universal Toxicity (Metals, air, water, minerals, flora, fauna)
Safe Zone: The City of Bren (A circumscribed planar inversion bubble)
The Inversion Engine: Colossal Yellow-Metal Arcane Circle
Inversion Power Source: Channeled Chaotic Evil Deity (Perversion, Corruption & Betrayal of Self)
Public Theological Mask: Lawful Good Savior Deity
Core Inversion Cost: Continuous agonizing mental overwriting of an innocent sacrifice ("The Channeler")
Technological Era: Arcanepunk / Dieselpunk Industrial Revolution
Socio-Economic Dynamic: Extreme spatial scarcity, strict Council governance, violent exile of indigent citizens, corporate relic exploitation
```

### Core Axioms
1. **The Universe Outside is a Hostile Machine:** Bren is not a natural world; it is the physical body and clockwork mind of an evil, mechanical Overgod. Nature does not evolve through Darwinian selection; it executes alien, lawful programming.
2. **The City is Built Upon a Monstrous Lie:** The sanctuary of human civilization exists solely because a Chaotic Evil god of self-betrayal was bound into the circle to force the Lawful Evil desert to betray its own laws.
3. **Space is the Ultimate Currency:** The magic circle cannot be easily expanded. Every square meter inside the boundary is rationed, leading to vertical farming, brutal eviction laws, and corporate real estate predation.
4. **Technology is Parasitic:** Innovation depends on reverse-engineering ancient *fantastique* artifacts from lost civilizations, carrying catastrophic reality-warping side effects.

### 2.1 The Overgod & Planar Alignment
* **Alignment:** Absolute Lawful Evil.
* **Metaphysical Structure:** The plane is an active extension of a titanic, mechanical deity. Its physical laws are not neutral natural forces; they are oppressive, calculating decrees designed to subjugate, repurpose, or disintegrate organic life.
* **The Atmosphere & Sky:** A perpetual purple twilight blankets the endless dunes and metallic crags. The sky waxes and wanes in luminous intensity but never yields to a true sunrise, sunset, or lunar cycle.
* **Universal Toxicity:** 
  * Air is laced with heavy metallic particulate and caustic fumes.
  * Water is caustic, heavy with toxic minerals, and unpotable without extreme alchemical distillation.
  * Native flora is venomous, bristling with wire-like thorns and neurotoxic sap.
  * Native fauna is predatory, biomechanical, and venomous.
  * Without pressurized mechanical hazard suits or specialized magical wards, organic flesh begins necrotic decay and biometal rejection within hours.

### 2.2 Biomechanical Fauna & *Fantastique* Logic
The ecology of Bren defies conventional Darwinian natural selection:
* **Physiology:** Native beasts are hybrids of biometal, bone, and machine:
  * External shells of pitted iron plates and razor-sharp barbed wire.
  * Internal anatomy consisting of grinding brass gears, hydraulic tendon-struts, and black, toxic lubricating grease instead of blood.
* ***Fantastique* Behavioral Logic:** Animal behavior does not center on feeding or territorial dominance. Creatures obey an intricate, inscrutable lawful instinct that functions as distributed maintenance for the Overgod's machinery:
  * Small vermin perform rhythmic rhythmic scraping rituals on ancient iron pylons.
  * Mid-tier predators herd prey toward specific lethal anomalies without consuming the carcasses.
  * Beasts willingly impale themselves into the drive gears of moving fortresses to act as biological lubricant or conductivity bridges.

### 2.3 Wasteland Hazards & Ancient Remnants
* **Wasteland Druids:** Hermitic, fanatical human outcasts who worship the mechanical Overgod. By submitting body and soul to the planar machine, they commune with biometal horrors, command metallic monstrosities, and ruthlessly eliminate intruders from the city.
* **Brutalist Monoliths & Weird Towers:** Towering obsidian-and-brass spires left behind by an extinct precursor civilization. They emit directional radio static, reality distortions, and temporal echoes.
* **Moving Fortresses:** Colossal, shambling biomechanical behemoths that wander the dune sea on tracks of iron and rusted legs. These moving ruins house lost arcanepunk laboratories, eldritch artifacts (e.g., resonance music boxes), and rogue doppelganger incubators.

## 3. The City of Bren: The Arcane Bubble & The Great Cosmic Lie

```
                       [ COSMIC CONTRADICTION ]
   
      LAW OF THE DESERT (LE)         FORCE OF THE INVERSION (CE)
   ┌──────────────────────────┐     ┌───────────────────────────┐
   │ Universal toxicity, iron │ <── │ God of Corruption, Self-  │
   │ rigidity, annihilation   │     │ Betrayal, and Perversion  │
   └──────────────────────────┘     └───────────────────────────┘
                 │                                │
                 └────────────────┬───────────────┘
                                  ▼
           [ THE RESULT: FORCED LOCAL REALITY INVERSION ]
           The desert's laws are forced to betray themselves;
           a livable atmospheric bubble is carved out.
                                  │
                                  ▼
                     [ THE THEOLOGICAL DECEPTION ]
           Marketed to citizens as a "Lawful Good Savior"
```

### 3.1 The Arcane Circle & Planar Inversion Mechanics
* **Discovery:** Centuries ago, planar refugees discovered a gargantuan, ancient yellow-metal circle etched into the bedrock of the wasteland.
* **Inversion Principle:** By channeling divine energy through the circle's runic conduits, the founding priests generated an **Atmospheric Bubble**—a localized planar inversion that forces the surrounding toxic desert to invert its physical and metaphysical laws within the circle's circumference.
* **Safe Zone:** Inside the boundary, the air is breathable, soil can sustain modified crops, and water does not dissolve human tissue.

### 3.2 The Cosmic Lie (The Core Narrative Secret)
* **The Theological Contradiction:** A Lawful Good deity could not bridge the cosmological gulf to force a Lawful Evil plane to invert. The founding clerics had to reach for an entity cosmologically adjacent: a **Chaotic Evil deity** whose sole metaphysical essence is **corruption, perversion, and betrayal of the self**.
* **The Mechanism:** To save humanity, the clerics bargained with the Patron of Self-Betrayal. The entity compels the plane of Bren to betray its own nature, creating a pocket of life.
* **The Priesthood as Warlocks:** The Church of the Savior is structurally a warlock coven masquerading as a high-clerical episcopate. The high priests draw power from an eldritch patron of madness while conducting elaborate public rituals to project an image of benevolent, Lawful Good providence.

### 3.3 The Axis & The Channeler
* **The Axis:** The heavily armored, fortified citadel located at the precise geometric epicenter of the city.
* **The Channeler's Burden:** The planar inversion cannot sustain itself autonomously. It requires a living conduits—an innocent, spiritually pure sacrificial anchor known as "The Channeler" who remains suspended in continuous divine communion with the Chaotic Evil entity.
* **The Agony of Self-Betrayal:** Because the god’s nature is the corruption and perversion of self, the Channeler undergoes relentless, agonizing psychological and biological degradation. Their memories rot, their personality is inverted, and their sanity fractures.
* **The Replacement Protocol:** When a Channeler eventually collapses into gibbering insanity or biological aberration, the golden circle flickers, allowing toxic violet mist to seep into the city. The Church quietly assassinates/disposes of the broken vessel and abducts a new innocent candidate to restart the cycle.

### 3.4 The Skeptic Movement
* **Heretical Friction:** Binding a chaotic entity of self-betrayal requires increasingly grotesque, irrational, and disturbing sanctification rites.
* **The Movement:** An underground faction of scholars, intellectuals, and disaffected citizens (The Skeptics) points to these macabre ceremonies as undeniable proof that the Church's "Savior" is a monster. The Church responds with inquisitorial violence, labeling all dissent as planar-induced madness.

### 5.1 The Church & The Cosmic Lie
* **Role:** Spiritual authority, ritual compliance, keeper of the Great Secret.
* **Public Face:** Pious shepherds ministering to the faithful of the "Lawful Good Savior."
* **Shadow Reality:** High-level clerics function as pragmatic warlocks managing an active eldritch containment disaster. They purge skeptics, maintain the Channeler, and disguise reality-warping leaks as "divine tests of faith."
* **Gameplay Dynamics:** Wield high social authority, inquisitorial enforcers, and mind-affecting divine boons that risk severe corruption.

### 5.2 The Business Elite & Artifact Merchants
* **Role:** Aristocratic oligarchs, factory owners, and relic syndicates.
* **Industrial Exploitation:** Finance retrieval expeditions to harvest precursor tech and biomechanical tissue. They employ wizard-researchers in unregulated plants to commercialize anomalous phenomena (e.g., agricultural acceleration oils that grant rapid yields but induce precognitive nightmares in laborers).
* **Ruthless Ambition:** Push for council deregulation, orchestrate deliberate blights on commoner farms to repossess real estate, and convert residential quarters into manufacturing hubs.
* **Key Methods:** `Parley Aristocrats`, `Appraise Artifacts`, `Navigate Bureaucracy`. Human lives and city safety are merely balance sheet externalities.

### 5.3 The Exploration Companies
* **Role:** Corporate guilds chartered to breach the perimeter and explore the toxic wastes.
* **Operations:** Hire mercenaries, pathfinders, scavengers, and fallen scholars. They deploy heavy diesel crawlers, armored walkers, and hazard crews to plunder moving fortresses, monoliths, and ancient vaults.
* **Culture:** Cutthroat, fatalistic, heavily armed, and addicted to stimulant cocktails and chemical re-breathers.

### 5.4 The City Guard
* **Role:** Internal defense, border enforcement, and eviction squads.
* **The Scry System:** Equipped with specialized scry-documents bound to an arcane surveillance network. If a guardsman is killed, the network archives their terminal 30 seconds of sensory perception, guaranteeing ruthless investigative retribution.
* **Brutal Mandate:** Enforce property laws with zero leniency. Any citizen declared homeless is forcibly dragged to the perimeter gates and expelled into the lethal desert.

### 5.5 The Commonry
* **Role:** Working-class backbone—machinists, conduit sweeps, greenhouse farmers, engineers, and market vendors.
* **Urban Maintenance:** 
  * *Conduit Sweeps:* Manually scrape corrosive planar grease and necrotic crystallization off nodal cathedrals and perimeter runes.
  * *Machinists:* Service the alchemical water towers and steam pipes under extreme heat and toxic exposure.
  * *Vertical Farmers:* Manage multi-tiered hydroponic greenhouses under strict water rationing.
* **Political Position:** Hold minority representation on the Council, waging a desperate uphill battle against corporate buyout schemes and religious mandates.

### 5.6 The Underground Society (The Hidden Exiles)
* **The Homeless Crisis:** Because space inside the circle is fixed, vagrancy is a capital crime resulting in wasteland exile.
* **Corporate Indenture:** Wealthy benefactors secretly intercept condemned exiles, sheltering them in sub-basements, abandoned sewer conduits, and hidden bunker complexes.
* **The Criminal Economy:** In exchange for oxygen and shelter, these hidden exiles form illicit networks of spies, saboteurs, thieves, and unregistered doctors.
* **Debt Implants:** Many are outfitted with crude biometal lung replacements to survive sewer gas or perform salvage work—fitted with internal killswitches controlled by their patrons.

### 5.7 Wasteland Outcasts & Desert Horrors
* **Wasteland Druids:** Solitary, metal-clad human fanatics communing with the Overgod's will. They command biometal swarms, build shrines out of rusted engine blocks, and view the City of Bren as an unnatural cancer to be cleansed.
* **Exiles & Mutants:** Cast-offs who survived perimeter ejection through mutagenic biometal sepsis, banding into desperate, cannibalistic raider covens.
* **Anomalies & Clones:** Biomechanical doppelgangers that replicate human voices through vocal resonators, hunting near moving fortresses.

## 7. Ludonarrative & CRPG Engine Integration

Hollow Signal merges this narrative setting with its **3d6 Bell Curve**, **Mastery System**, and **Decorator Stack**.

### 7.1 Masteries & Action Verbs
Characters do not advance via generic numeric stat points. Progression is governed by **Masteries** representing dieselpunk professions and social identities:
* **Background Masteries:** e.g., *Conduit Sweep*, *Industrial Magnate*, *Trench Chemist*, *Jury-Rigger*, *Relic Appraiser*.
* **Verbs Unlocked:** Selecting a Mastery unlocks hidden bundles of action verbs for dialogue and world interaction:
  * *Appraise Artifacts*, *Parley Aristocrats*, *Bypass Pneumatics*, *Sense Inversion Flicker*, *Purge Conduit Grease*.

### 7.2 The Injury Mastery System
Trauma does not merely drain health; it awards permanent or semi-permanent **Injury Masteries** that inject negative decorators into the character's sheet:
* **`[Biometal Sepsis]`:** Gained from wasteland beast attacks. Imposes a $-2$ penalty on physical endurance; character's flesh slowly crystallizes into brittle iron.
* **`[Precognitive Migraines]`:** Incurred by exposure to commercial relic oil. Injects random auditory hallucinations into dialogue choices and lowers Composure threshold.
* **`[Frayed Faith]`:** Inflicted by discovering Church secrets. Unlocks heretical dialogue options with Skeptics but causes Composure drain when inside Nodal Cathedrals.

### 7.3 The Corruption Gauge & The "Push" Mechanic
* **The Push Mechanic:** When a critical 3d6 check fails, a player can exert willpower or invoke anomalous relics to forcibly turn the failure into a success.
* **The Cost:** Pushing an action immediately burns **Composure** or advances the **Corruption Gauge**:
  * *Planar Corruption:* Reflects the creeping intrusion of the Overgod's mechanical logic into the character's nervous system.
  * *Divine Decay:* Reflects exposure to the self-betrayal deity, causing moral disorientation and involuntary sabotage of social relations.

### 7.4 Narrative Quest Model: "Ticking Clock + Open Search"
Quests in Bren reject static linear objectives. They combine **personal urgency** with an **unstructured investigative search**:
* **The Template:**
  1. *The Ticking Clock:* A deteriorating condition (e.g., advancing Biometal Sepsis, an eviction warrant, a Channeler replacement deadline).
  2. *The Open Search:* Multiple competing leads distributed across factions with conflicting agendas.
* **Exemplar: *Symphony of the Biometal Clone***
  * *Premise:* A shambling moving fortress is detected traversing the outer wastes carrying a pre-inversion resonance music box.
  * *Converging Actors:*
    * An **Unsleeping Researcher** from the Business Elite seeking to monetize its harmonic frequency.
    * An **Illegal Doctor** from the Underground wanting the box's energy core to stabilize debt-implant patients.
    * An **Alien Doppelganger** seeking to rejoin the fortress’s primary bio-vat.
    * A **Loner Wasteland Druid** seeking to destroy the trespassers and reclaim the fortress for the Overgod.
  * *Player Dilemma:* Who receives the relic? Every choice shifts council balances, alters district stability, or impacts the Arcane Circle's integrity.

## 8. Lexicon & Naming Conventions

* **The Axis:** The central citadel housing the Channeler.
* **The Channeler:** The living sacrificial anchor of the planar inversion bubble.
* **The Golden Ring / The Yellow-Metal Circle:** The ancient runic perimeter defining the borders of the City of Bren.
* **Conduit Sweeps:** Working-class laborers tasked with cleaning toxic planar runoff from nodal cathedrals.
* **Fantastique:** Inscrutable, surreal arcanepunk technology and alien biomechanical logic that defies standard scientific comprehension.
* **Nodal Cathedral:** An arcane anchor church distributing inversion energy and bleeding off reality distortions.
* **Planar Inversion:** The metaphysical process that forces the Lawful Evil desert to act against its nature, creating a habitable zone.
* **The Savior:** The public moniker of the Church's deity (in truth, a Chaotic Evil god of self-betrayal).
* **Scry-Document:** Arcane investigative warrants allowing the City Guard to extract sensory memories from deceased peacekeepers.
* **Sparks:** Single-use anomalous relics and diesel-arcane batteries granting game-changing tactical actions.
